About Me

Hey there! I'm an M.Sc. student in Robotics, Systems and Control at ETH Zürich, with a B.Sc. in Vehicle Engineering from KTH. My current research with Dr. Tao Du’s group at Tsinghua IIIS focuses on sim-to-real development for a biomimetic flapping-wing robot, where I contribute across embedded wireless control, SBUS communication, experimental data acquisition, fuselage geometry reconstruction, and experimental fixture design.

I'm interested in physics-based modeling and simulation, physics-informed learning, decision-making and optimal control, and sim-to-real robotics.

Outside my studies and research, I enjoy hiking, skiing, jogging, and other outdoor activities. I like spending time in nature and exploring new places. I also spent two years as a professional PUBG Mobile player, winning the PUBG Mobile Pro League Western Europe Spring 2021 in a 16-team professional field and competing internationally.
