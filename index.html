About

Hey there! I'm an M.Sc. student in Robotics, Systems and Control at ETH Zürich, with a B.Sc. in Vehicle Engineering from KTH. My current research with Dr. Tao Du's group at Tsinghua IIIS focuses on sim-to-real development for a biomimetic flapping-wing robot, including embedded wireless control, SBUS communication, and experimental data acquisition.

I'm interested in physics-based modeling and simulation, optimal control, physics-informed learning, and sim-to-real robotics.
